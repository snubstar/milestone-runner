### One interesting non-Europe-specific cash grant

**LINGUA Africa** is backed by Microsoft AI for Good Lab, Gates Foundation, Masakhane, and Google.org. It is open until **15 June 2026** and offers combinations of **cash + Azure/GCP credits**: suggested awards range from **$50k cash + $50k compute** for data creation to **$250k cash + $400k compute** for sectoral applications. It is Africa-focused, but organizations outside Africa may apply if there is a meaningful African partnership. ([Microsoft][14])

([Google Org][15]) OpenAI’s **EMEA Youth & Wellbeing Grant** and **People-First AI Fund** are also closed, and Meta’s public Llama startup/impact grant pages I found are either closed or US-first rather than a current Europe/Italy route.
